# openSandbox
Open Sandbox App

Privacy Policy:
We do not take any data from the user, and we only look at the files we created in Google Drive. No personal data is sent back to any serve beside's Google's own, and all information is routed via the user's google account. 

Terms of Service:
Our terms of service stem from Google Colab's terms of service, found [here]([url](https://research.google.com/colaboratory/tos_v6.html)). If you agree to their terms of service, and do not intend to maliciously use this app, you will have satisfied our terms of service.
